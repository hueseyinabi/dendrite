This blog is the successor to my "synapse" app that used to be only available to me. I have now found a workflow that allows me to share my notes with friends and is also easier to maintain. _Dendrite_ is a [digital garden](https://de.wikipedia.org/wiki/Digital_Gardening) and a derivative of _synapse_ (my private notebook). This setup allows me to share specific notes, so I can make sure they do not contain sensitive or uncited information.

Due to the way this is set up, you need to press **[this button](https://hueseyinabi.github.io/dendrite/notes/dendrite.html)** to see the actual content. However do *not* press [this](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dQw4w9WgXcQ) button under any circumstances... funny things might happen!
